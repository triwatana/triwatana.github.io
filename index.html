TasteMatch CA — Finding Your Next Favorite

Jan. 2026 – Apr. 2026 · team of 3

What if finding a restaurant felt more like asking a friend?

A hybrid restaurant recommender for Santa Barbara, built with two teammates. It combines semantic search, content-based filtering, and collaborative filtering, and surfaces the restaurants that several models agree on.

  • My part: built the TF-IDF content-based filtering model over 1,036 restaurants, computing cosine similarity across all restaurant pairs on category, price, and attribute features.
  • Teammates built the ChromaDB semantic search (all-MiniLM-L6-v2 embeddings, HNSW index) and the ALS collaborative filter trained on ~44,000 Yelp reviews.
  • The combined system reached 0.783 Precision@5 and 0.833 NDCG@5 (team evaluation).
